## 2026-10-15 — M25 durable operation queue and safe send

Gmail triage is now local-first. `thread.update`, `thread.undo` and reminders change the thread at once and, in the same SQLite transaction, add a provider change to a new `ops` table (schema v2). A worker sends ops in order per account and holds back that account's later ops when one fails, retrying after 30 s doubling to 15 min. "Sync now" retries at once, and a 404 drops the op because the thread is gone. Sync sends queued ops before it pulls, and threads with an op still queued keep their local status, read state, star and labels, so a pull can no longer undo what you just did. Settings shows how many changes are still waiting to reach the mail host. Closing the app waits for a running flush; anything left goes out after the next start. Disconnecting an account drops its ops.

Sending can no longer go out twice. Each draft gets one Message-ID on the sender's domain at its first send, kept for retries; `sendAttemptAt` is saved before Gmail is called. A network error or 5xx is treated as uncertain: Aplos searches Sent mail for that Message-ID and finishes the send if it is there, otherwise the draft keeps an error asking you to check Sent mail. A 4xx rejection is reported as is. On startup, a draft that was mid-send is settled only from Sent mail and is never resent automatically.
