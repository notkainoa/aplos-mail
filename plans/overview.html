The look: Paper

Quiet gray and white after Notion and Redakt, roomy type, ink blue for the one main action, a serif for AI text. No sky, no airmail stripe, no page titles, no app icon until the new logo.

Every screen is drawn in the mockups: open mockups/index.html in this repo, or aplos-mockups.vercel.app. Home is now Do now, Important updates, Low importance and Follow up. DESIGN.md has the full spec.
