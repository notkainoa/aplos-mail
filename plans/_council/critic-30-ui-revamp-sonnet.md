# Critique of plan 30 (UI revamp), Sonnet critic

Checked against DESIGN.md (primary), ARCHITECTURE.md, plans 10/20/40, `research/pr21-onboarding-review.md`, PR #21 itself (`gh pr view 21`) and Aplos main `72095ff` (read-only). Counts in the plan's section 2 re-verified (8,534 renderer lines, 53 `invoke(` sites, 56 `@keyframes`, hold timings 300/250/220, no `lint` script): all correct. Plan states 14 PR #21 findings; DESIGN Appendix C and the review agree (F1-F14), and every one is mapped.

## Blockers

1. **blocker. The live-windows flip is parked at U9 (weeks 11-13), but plan 10 needs it at E3 (week 6).** Plan 10 E3 item 8 flips the UI from fixture to real engine "by flag" once the real engine passes the conformance suite, E3 deletes `latestState` (weeks 4-6, not "at U9"), and E8 shim removal (weeks 10-11, `assembleLegacyState`, legacy `state.get`) waits on "plan 30 reports the renderer fully on windows". As written, E8 and ARCH's 0.4.0 wait for U9, which ends two to three weeks after the engine. Also `compat.ts` (a renderer-side window emulator) is redundant because plan 10 E1 deliverable 6 already ships a fixture that serves windows. Fix: flip as a gate right after E3 acceptance (a work package in U5); `compat.ts` becomes a contingency only if E3 slips more than two weeks; U9 keeps cleanup, budgets and accessibility.

2. **blocker. The owner's three onboarding wishes are not reflected; one conflicts with DESIGN.** DESIGN 7.11 puts "Look around with demo mail" as a second Welcome button; the owner wants a small top-right button that loads demo data only on click. Today `service.ts:195` seeds demo data on every fresh store (`store.read()||createDemo(now())`), and neither plan 10 (E2 import, E5 `Setup/*`) nor plan 30 says a fresh install starts empty or that demo loading is a contract call. PR #21 already does this (title: "demo only on request"), so absorbing it must keep that behaviour. The "full-bleed like Arc, Dia or Zen" wish is only partly met (640x600 resizable card on a sky; no mention of traffic lights over the scene). Fix in U8, section 4.5 and section 8.1.

3. **blocker. First-sync screen is not consistent with the Gmail quota.** DESIGN 7.12 shows "Found 214 recent emails" and "Reading the newest 120 of 214", which implies a mailbox total that Gmail does not give cheaply and which the 6,000 units/minute budget makes impossible to read quickly. ARCH 5.1 reads a first window of about 50 threads as metadata, commits per 50, then backfills for minutes; plan 20 sets `baseline_done_at` at the end of P2. The plan never defines what "ready" means. Fix: the gate is the first window only; counts are estimates worded "about"; backfill is never part of the gate; a rate-limit pause before the first batch still lets the owner enter; and the Home-empty "all clear" sky moment must not fire while a backfill or pause is running.

## Majors

4. **major. Timeline not reconciled.** Plan uses ARCH M-numbers and never says which engine milestone each U needs; plan 10 uses E-numbers (ARCH M4 is E3 plus E4, M9 is E7). U0 at weeks 0-1 contradicts its own day-zero gate (Q1 format commit, E1 freeze window on the first days of week 1, E0 0.2.4). Estimates sum to 64-94 days (13-19 weeks serial) against a 12-13 week claim with no pessimistic case. The UI ends about week 13 while ARCH 0.4.0 is week 11 and the plan says the dogfood week precedes 0.4.0 without saying who moves. Fix: dependency table in E-numbers, retimed weeks, DESIGN stage mapping, a pessimistic range, and a single 0.4.0 plan (soak and dogfood on the same release candidate).

5. **major. U8 "merge the PR head" is unsafe.** PR #21 touches 46 files including `engine/service.ts` (+90), `google.ts`, `ports.ts`, `provider.ts`, `shared/types.ts`, `preload.ts`, `server.ts`, and `tests/onboarding.test.ts` (engine tests). Merging it collides with plan 10's `service.ts` split and `Setup/*` rewrite and plan 20's Google files. Fix: take only renderer and window files by path; engine tests are re-targeted to `Setup/*` by plan 10 E5.

6. **major. Duplicate work on call sites.** Plan 10 E1 runs a codemod over about 60 renderer call sites inside a freeze window; U2 says it migrates all 53. Fix: plan 10 owns the mechanical codemod, U2 verifies it and builds the data layer; U0 must not touch call sites during the window.

7. **major. U0 is not complete or explicit.** It covers items 1-8, 16, 17 of DESIGN 13 in prose, with no 17-row accounting, and defers 9-15 to U3 (week 5) while plan 40's provenance gate (plan 40 line 447) assumes all 17 gone at stage 1. The root cause, `DECISIONS.md:125` and `:129` (visual and interaction parity), is superseded only in U9, so agents reading DECISIONS.md for nine milestones are told to copy Avec. DESIGN's line numbers predate plan 40's format commit and will shift. Class names and constants (`.dock-pill`, `.deck.has-next`, `.low-line`) are not in the provenance grep. Fix: explicit table, supersession in U0, locate by string, an optional interim identity swap.

8. **major. Ownership and path clashes with plan 40.** Plan 30 puts lint plugins in `apps/desktop/lint/`; plan 40 reserves `tools/lint/`. Plan 30's `tests/ui/` Playwright setup (U0) precedes plan 40's single pinned Playwright base. Plan 40 owns root devDependencies. Plan 30 does not hand over `check:tokens` and `check:identity`. Plan 10 E1 says plan 30 supplies the menu-bar glyph; plan 30 never does.

## Minors

9. **minor.** Broken reference "section 4.5 row 11" (the table has 7 rows). U4 acceptance repeats its intro line. F1/F9 tests need the Settings "Show welcome" re-entry path, which the plan never keeps. Hands-on checks omit the onboarding window chrome (traffic lights, resize, drag region), which a browser-preview harness cannot see. The timing spike should also prove `page.screenshot` on a `show:false` Electron window.

10. **minor (owner readability).** "Decisions for you" sits at the end and uses prose lists, not the options/recommended/what-happens table of the other plans. "In plain English" says nothing about onboarding or how the 13 weeks relate to the engine. Fix: move it up, tabulate, add decisions for onboarding look, auto-enter, and release timing.

11. **minor. Headless rule.** Satisfied (layers 1-6 use Playwright and hidden `_electron`; no visible-window automation). Keep `APLOS_TEST=1` as a hard dependency in section 8.1 and state that the onboarding window is created `show:false` under test.
