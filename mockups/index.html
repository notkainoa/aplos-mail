Aplos, after the revamp

Revision 2: calmer, neutral, more room. What changed is in DESIGN-CHANGES.md.

Static mockups of every planned screen of the revamped Aplos. Click a picture to open the screen at real window size. Each screen has its own states, listed under it. Light and dark use the switch on the right, and every screen remembers it.

Inside a screen: J and K move, E is Done, L is Later, Z undoes, Return opens, Space runs the suggested action, ⌘K opens commands, / searches, ? shows every key, and Esc goes back. Hold ⌘ to see key hints. The small bar at the bottom right of each screen switches theme and state.

Mockups only: no real data, no real people. Values DESIGN.md left open are listed in NOTES.md. Screenshots are made with node mockups/shoot.cjs.
